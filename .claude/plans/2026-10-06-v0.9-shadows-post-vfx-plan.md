### 1.4 What games do instead (the code v0.9 retires)

- **Candlewick**, `Wardens::ClampRange` (`Wardens.cpp:562-583`): every frame each warden's eye
  light is shortened to the first wall its facing ray hits, because light passes through walls. The
  C4 log calls it out: "The engine has no shadows until v0.9". `--no-range-clamp` exists only to
  show the leak.
- **Candlewick**, `IsFlameLit` (`Detection.cpp:108-132`) counts a player behind a pillar as lit by
  the brazier on the far side. There is no "hide in the shadow" today.
- **Candlewick** flame peaks are held near 1.2 to limit clipping (`candlewick-plan.md:352`, K17);
  flames are emissive spheres flickered by two sines (`Flicker.h:9-12`, `Braziers.cpp:183-202`).
- **Candlewick** hides wall pieces for the camera cutaway with `Visible = false`
  (`KeepWorld.cpp:465-494`). With shadows, a hidden wall must still cast, or the light leaks the
  moment the camera looks past it.
- **Marionette**: an impact is a sound at the target's position, 0.07 s hit-stop and a react clip
  (`Combat.cpp:151-205`). No spark, no flash, no footfall dust. `step_l` / `step_r` play a sound
  only (`Fighter.cpp:1023-1056`). Its brazier flames are unflickered emissive spheres.
- `docs/lighting.md:373-385` (Limits): "No tone mapping or HDR until v0.9", "No shadows until v0.9".

## 2. Design

The rule every earlier milestone kept stays: **a scene that doesn't ask for a new feature renders
exactly as before** (0 px, proven against a deliberately broken build). Shadows are opt-in per
light, the post chain is opt-in per camera, particles exist only where an emitter does (D2).

### 2.1 Profiling (first, so each pass is measured as it lands)

**Tracy** (D1), vendored as a submodule (`vendor/tracy`, a KingoBoiii fork like the others, pinned
to a release). It is compiled in only with a premake option, `--profile`, which defines
`DE_PROFILE`, `TRACY_ENABLE` and `TRACY_ON_DEMAND` (nothing is streamed until the Tracy viewer
connects). Without it every macro is empty; CI keeps its Debug/Release/Distribution matrix and
gains one Release `--profile` build so it keeps compiling.

**Public API** (`include/DingoEngine/Core/Profiler.h`), Tracy-free to keep the backend-hiding rule:

```cpp
DE_PROFILE_FRAME();                 // once per frame, in Application::Run
DE_PROFILE_SCOPE("Scene::OnUpdate");
DE_PROFILE_FUNCTION();
DE_PROFILE_PLOT("Particles alive", count);
DE_PROFILE_THREAD("Render");
```

A scope expands to a `static constexpr Dingo::ProfileSite { name, function, file, line, color }`,
laid out exactly like Tracy's `___tracy_source_location_data` (a `static_assert` in `src/` checks
it), and an RAII `Dingo::ProfileZone` whose constructor and destructor live in `src/` and call
Tracy's C API. One out-of-line call per zone edge with profiling on, nothing with it off.

**Engine zones**: the frame, every layer's `OnUpdate`, `AssetManager::Update`, `Scene::OnUpdate`
and each of its systems, `SceneRenderer::Render`, `Renderer3D::EndScene` and its steps (lights,
uploads, shadows, main pass, particles), each post pass, `Renderer2D` flushes, ImGui, and the
render thread's execute/present (thread named "Render").

**GPU pass times, in the engine** (they work without Tracy): `Renderer::BeginGpuTimer("Shadows")` /
`EndGpuTimer()` on NVRHI timer queries, a ring of 4 frames so a read never waits. Each pass of §2.3–
§2.10 is wrapped. Results go to a new **F8 Profiler** tab (GPU ms per pass, 120-frame mean and max,
next to the CPU frame / update / render split) and to Tracy as plots.

### 2.2 Groundwork: render targets, pipeline state, readback, includes

Small items, each with a default equal to today's behaviour so every existing pipeline is built
exactly as before:

- **Formats**: `TextureFormat` gains `RGBA16F`, `R11G11B10F`, `R8`, `R16F`, `R32F` and `D32`
  (mapped through the existing `Format` mirror). Upload pitch comes from the format.
- **Sampled depth**: `FramebufferParams::SetDepthSampleable(true)` creates the depth typeless with
  an SRV; `Framebuffer::GetDepthAttachment()` returns it as a `Texture*`. The swap chain's depth
  stays as it is.
- **Framebuffer fixes**, needed before a post chain can hold attachment pointers: `Resize`
  reinitializes the same `Texture` objects in place (as hot-reload already does), `GetWidth/
  GetHeight` are right from creation, attachments are created once, and `Clear` handles a
  depth-only framebuffer.
- **Viewport and scissor**: `Renderer::SetViewport(rect)` for the current target (shadow-atlas
  tiles, half-resolution passes). `Renderer::GetRenderTarget()` becomes public.
- **Pipeline state**: `BlendMode { Alpha (default), Additive, Opaque }`, `DepthCompare { Less
  (default), LessEqual, Greater, Always }`, `DepthBias` and `SlopeScaledDepthBias`, `DepthClip`,
  and a vertex-only shader (no fragment stage) for depth-only pipelines.
- **Samplers**: per-axis address modes, `Border` with a border colour, and `CompareMode::LessEqual`
  for hardware PCF. `Sampler::Create` and `RenderPass::Create` initialize like every other factory.
- **Fullscreen passes**: `Renderer::Draw(RenderPass*, vertexCount)`, and an engine-internal
  `FullscreenPass` (one triangle from `gl_VertexIndex`, a material, a target).
- **Readback**: `Renderer::ReadTexture(texture, rect, callback)` copies to a staging texture and
  calls back a few frames later, never stalling. Tests can finally check pixels, and §2.8's shadow
  probes use the same path.
- **`#include`** (shaderc includer): `#include "Shadows.glsl"` resolves next to the including file,
  `#include <DingoEngine/Shadows.glsl>` resolves to an engine shader, from `DE_ENGINE_SHADER_DIR` in
  Debug and from an index of the embedded shaders otherwise. Included bytes join the cache hash;
  a hot-reload watches every included file. Bump `k_ShaderCacheFormatVersion` to 3.
- **Vulkan**: enable `shaderClipDistance` (universal on desktop GPUs; §2.5 needs it).
- **Tests**: one shared `Check(bool, name)` helper for the test app (eight tests copy it today), and
  a **Render Target Test** (`--test=rt`) that clears and reads back every new format, samples a
  depth attachment, and resizes a framebuffer under a live render pass.

### 2.3 The post chain and tone mapping

**Settings** (`Graphics/PostProcess.h`, public):

```cpp
struct PostProcessSettings
{
    bool Enabled = false;
    ToneMapSettings Tone;        // Operator, Exposure (EV), the Soft knee and white point
    BloomSettings Bloom;         // §2.4
    AmbientOcclusionSettings AO; // §2.9
};
```

**ECS**: a `PostProcessComponent { PostProcessSettings Settings; }` on the primary camera's entity
(registered in `DE_INSTANTIATE_COMPONENT` and the duplicate list). **Without the ECS**: a
`PostProcessStack` (`Renderer::GetPostProcessStack()`), used as
`stack.Begin(settings)` → draw 3D with `Renderer3D` → `stack.End()`. `SceneRenderer` uses the same
object.

**The frame with post enabled**:

```
caller's target (swap chain or the test viewport)
  └ Begin: scene target = RGBA16F colour + sampleable D32, sized to the caller's target
      shadow atlas (§2.5) → opaque + skinned → AO multiply (§2.9) → particles (§2.11)
  └ End: bloom (§2.4) → tone map + exposure → caller's target
  └ 2D overlay (Renderer2D, unchanged, LDR, never bloomed or tone mapped)
```

With `Enabled = false` the 3D pass draws straight into the caller's target as today, so existing
scenes are untouched. The scene target is cached per output size and reinitialized in place on
resize. `SceneRenderer` saves and restores the caller's target and takes its aspect from it
(today a scene rendered into the test viewport takes the window's aspect).

**Tone mapping** (D3). The engine stays display-referred in v0.9: textures, lights and the lit
shader keep their meaning, and the tone map only decides what happens above 1.0.

| Operator | Below 1.0 | Above 1.0 | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| `None` | identity | clips (today) | proving the chain changes nothing |
| `Soft` **(default)** | identity up to the knee (0.8) | rolls off smoothly to the white point (default 4.0 → 1.0), on the max channel so hue holds | every existing game: its unclipped pixels keep their v0.8 values, #76 is fixed |
| `ACES` | filmic toe and contrast | filmic shoulder | a game re-tuned for it |
| `Neutral` | Khronos PBR Neutral (subtracts a small offset, so darks shift) | compresses | a game re-tuned for it |

Exposure is a multiplier in EV, applied before the curve. Blending in 16F differs from 8-bit by
rounding, so "post on, `None`" is ≤ 1/255 from "post off" on translucent pixels, not 0 px;
"post off" is the 0 px gate.

**Test**: a **Post Test** (`--test=post`, `--post=tonemap|bloom|ao|chain`). Readback checks: each
curve is monotonic, `Soft` is identity below its knee to 1/255, `None` matches post-off on opaque
pixels, an overbright gradient keeps its hue under `Soft`.

### 2.4 Bloom

The dual-filter bloom from Jimenez's *Next Generation Post Processing in Call of Duty: Advanced
Warfare* (2014): a 13-tap downsample with a Karis average on the first level (no fireflies), a
3×3 tent upsample, six levels from half resolution in R11G11B10F, added to the scene before the
tone map.

`BloomSettings { Enabled, Intensity = 0.5, Threshold = 1.0, Knee = 0.1, Radius }`. With the
default threshold only what would have clipped blooms, so a scene inside 0–1 gains nothing. Lit
materials' `EmissiveStrength` above 1 is the intended source: Candlewick's braziers, Marionette's
flames, EchoVault's orbs.

### 2.5 Shadows: the shared machinery

**One shadow atlas**: a sampleable D32 texture, 4096² by default (64 MB;
`Renderer3DParams::Shadows.AtlasSize` 2048–8192). Cascades and local lights get square tiles from a
quadtree allocator rebuilt every scene. The shader samples one texture through one comparison
sampler, so the abstraction needs no texture arrays or cube maps.

**Every shadow view in one draw per batch.** `EndScene` already holds the scene's static batches in
world space. After uploading them, it draws each batch once more with `instanceCount` = the number
of shadow views, into the atlas, through one engine depth-only pipeline (vertex-only, slope-scaled
bias). The vertex stage takes view `gl_InstanceIndex`, transforms by that view's matrix from a
`ShadowViews` UBO (written once per scene), maps the result into the view's tile and writes four
clip distances at the tile's edges so nothing spills into a neighbour. Skinned meshes do the same
with `Renderer3D_Lit.glsl` built `DE_SKINNED` + `DE_SHADOW`. The atlas must be complete before the
first lit draw samples it, so a skinned instance uploads its palette twice a frame, once for the
shadow pass and once for the main pass; the `SkinData` write budget doubles to match.
Cost: every batch's vertices × the number of views (§7); per-mesh culling is v1.0's work.

*Fallback* if clip distances or the instanced path misbehave on a backend: one draw per view with
the viewport set to the tile and the view matrix in a volatile UBO (static batches), and one palette
upload per skinned instance per view.

**Sampling**: `Shadows.glsl` (an engine include) holds `ShadowFactor(worldPos, normal, ...)`:
hardware 2×2 PCF through the comparison sampler, a 3×3 kernel (16 effective taps), normal-offset
bias scaled by the tile's texel size, a border that reads lit. The lit shader multiplies it into the
directional and local terms. Scenes with no shadow caster skip it (`ShadowCounts == 0`), so their
pixels are unchanged.

**Bindings**: a `ShadowData` UBO (cascade matrices and splits, local shadow records, a per-local-
light shadow index), `u_ShadowAtlas` and `u_ShadowSampler`, found **by name** as `SkinData` is. The
lit shader takes bindings 5–7 (it already refuses slots past 0; `SkinData` is 4). A custom shader
receives shadows by `#include <DingoEngine/Shadows.glsl>` and declaring the same names at free
bindings (UBO ≤ 13, sampler ≤ 15 for D3D11). A shader that doesn't declare them is unaffected.

**Who casts**: `MeshRendererComponent` and `SkinnedMeshRendererComponent` gain
`ShadowCasting { On (default), Off, ShadowsOnly }`. `ShadowsOnly` draws into the atlas only: what
Candlewick's cut-away walls need. A chunk key gains the shadow mode; `Renderer3D::SubmitMesh` takes
it as an optional argument. Particles never cast. Lit materials receive; every material casts
through the engine's own depth pipeline, so a custom vertex shader's displacement isn't in its
shadow (documented).

### 2.6 Directional shadows (cascaded)

- `DirectionalLightComponent` and `DirectionalLight` gain `CastShadows = false` and
  `ShadowStrength = 1`. The first casting directional light gets the cascades; another warns once.
- `Renderer3DParams::Shadows`: `CascadeCount` 1–4 (4), `CascadeResolution` (1024), `MaxDistance`
  (60 m), `SplitLambda` (0.75, the practical split scheme), `CascadeBlend` (10 %), `DepthBias`,
  `SlopeBias`, `NormalBias`.
- **Stable cascades**: each is fitted to its frustum slice's bounding sphere (rotation does nothing)
  and snapped to whole texels (translation doesn't shimmer). The depth range reaches back toward
  the light to the bounds of everything submitted this scene (`SubmitMesh` already touches every
  vertex; it keeps a world AABB).
- An orthographic camera gets one cascade fitted to its view box.
- **Debug**: a cascade-colour overlay and an atlas viewer in F4.

### 2.7 Spot and point shadows (the bounded subset)

- `PointLightComponent`, `SpotLightComponent`, `PointLight` and `SpotLight` gain `CastShadows =
  false` and `ShadowStrength = 1`, appended so aggregate and constructor initialisation still work.
- **The cap is the design**: `Renderer3DCapabilities::MaxShadowedLocalLights` (default 8, at most
  16). After `ResolveSceneLights` picks the lights to draw, the casting ones take shadow slots in
  that same rank order. A spot light takes one tile, a point light six (one per cube face, each a
  little wider than 90° so PCF doesn't seam). Tile size by rank (1024 / 512 / 256), held for a
  light until its rank moves by two, so a still scene never changes. A light past the cap still
  lights, unshadowed: warn once, `Statistics::UnshadowedLights`, a row in F4.
- Spot: a perspective view from the cone (outer angle plus a margin), near 0.05, far = `Range`.
  Point: six views; the shader picks the face from the major axis of light → fragment.
- **#75 (K16), the rank-fade band** (D6): the light selection is open for the shadow slots anyway.
  A light ranked within the last few places of the budget fades by rank instead of switching off,
  and the same band decides a shadow slot's hand-over. Candlewick keeps its own LOD; this is for
  every other game.

### 2.8 Shadow probes: "is this point in shadow?" (D4)

v0.7's gameplay promise was that the cone you see is the cone that catches you, so
`GetLightAttenuation` runs the renderer's own maths. The v0.9 version: **the shadow you see is the
shadow that hides you.** A physics ray can't keep it: it tests colliders, not drawn meshes, and
knows nothing of bias, PCF, cascades or a light that lost its shadow slot this frame.

**Design**: a probe pass after the shadow atlas is drawn. Probe points go into a UBO; a fullscreen-
style draw into an N × 1 R8 target evaluates `ShadowFactor` for probe i against its light, the same
function the lit shader runs; the target is read back (§2.2). Answers arrive 2–3 frames late,
never stall, and a light without a shadow slot answers "lit", which is what is drawn.

```cpp
// Scene: re-issue every frame you care; reads the latest answer for this (light, key).
float Scene::GetLightVisibility(Entity light, glm::vec3 point, uint32_t key = 0);
float Scene::GetShadowedLightAttenuation(Entity light, glm::vec3 point, uint32_t key = 0);
// Without the ECS: Renderer3D::AddShadowProbe(lightIndex, point) / GetShadowProbeResult(id)
```

Budget: 256 probes a scene (warn once). The **Shadow Test** checks probes against a known occluder.

### 2.9 Ambient occlusion

Scalable Ambient Obscurance (McGuire et al. 2012) from the scene depth: view-space positions from
depth, normals from depth derivatives (best-of-two neighbours, so edges don't halo), 12 spiral
samples at half resolution into R8, a two-pass depth-aware blur, then a multiply on the HDR colour
after the opaque pass and before particles. Unity's forward post stack does the same; "ambient
only" needs a depth pre-pass or a G-buffer (§2.14).

`AmbientOcclusionSettings { Enabled, Radius = 0.5 m, Intensity = 1, Power = 1.5, Bias,
HalfResolution = true }`. Needs only the scene's projection and the sampleable depth from §2.3.

### 2.10 Compute, storage buffers and instancing

The prerequisite for GPU particles, and v1.0's instancing will use it too:

- **Compute shaders**: `#type compute` through shaderc → SPIR-V, and SPIRV-Cross → HLSL `cs_5_0`
  / `cs_5_1` for DXBC; `ComputePipeline` and `CommandList::Dispatch(x, y, z)`. Barriers stay
  NVRHI's automatic ones.
- **Storage buffers**: `GraphicsBufferType::Storage` with a stride; SRV and UAV views; the state
  mapping restored, `canHaveUAVs` set, the Vulkan UAV/SRV binding offsets set so GLSL `binding = N`
  lands; `RenderPass::SetStorageBuffer`. A vertex stage reads a storage buffer **read-only** (an SRV;
  D3D11 has no vertex-stage UAVs); writes happen in compute.
- **Instancing**: `Renderer::DrawIndexed(..., instanceCount)` exposed (also used by §2.5).
- **Test**: a **Compute Test** (`--test=compute`): a kernel fills a buffer and a texture, read back
  and checked on all three backends.

### 2.11 GPU particles (D5)

**Model**:

- A `ParticleEffect` is the look and behaviour, built with `ParticleEffectParams`: shape (point,
  sphere, cone, box), rate and bursts, lifetime range, speed range, gravity, drag, inherited
  emitter velocity, curl-noise strength and scale, size and rotation over life, colour over life
  (up to 4 HDR keys, so a spark can bloom), blend (`Additive`, `Alpha`), an optional sprite texture
  with flipbook frames, and the soft-particle fade distance.
- A `ParticleEmitter` is one running instance: a range of the GPU pool, a transform, a rate
  accumulator and a burst queue.

**On the GPU**: one engine-owned pool per `Renderer3D` (`Renderer3DCapabilities::MaxParticles`,
65,536 by default) in a storage buffer. Each emitter owns a ring in it, sized to what its effect can
have alive (`rate × max lifetime + largest burst`). Per scene, the CPU writes one spawn record per
emitter that spawns (transform, count, ring head, seed) and dispatches **emit** (initialises the new
slots; the CPU owns ring heads, so no atomics) and **simulate** (ages and integrates every live
slot). The draw is one instanced call per emitter, instance count = ring size: the vertex stage
reads its slot, collapses dead slots to nothing, and billboards the quad. Additive needs no sort;
alpha is unsorted (documented). Particles are unlit; colours above 1 bloom.

**Soft particles** read the scene depth, so they need the post chain; without it particles draw
into the frame after the 3D pass with a hard edge.

**ECS**: `ParticleEmitterComponent { ParticleEffect* Effect; bool Playing = true; float RateScale =
1; bool WorldSpace = true; }` (in `DE_INSTANTIATE_COMPONENT` and the duplicate list) and an engine-
internal `ParticleEmitterRuntime` in `RuntimeComponents.h`, like `AnimatorRuntime`. The emitter's
position comes from `HierarchySystem`, so an emitter on a socket follows a joint. API:
`Scene::EmitParticles(entity, count)`, `Scene::EmitParticlesAt(entity, position, count)` (an
impact point), `Play`/`Stop`. The simulation step is the scene's capped `dt`, accumulated until the
scene renders; a paused or skipped scene freezes its particles.

**Without the ECS**: `Renderer3D::CreateParticleEmitter(effect)` and `SubmitParticles(emitter,
transform, dt)` per scene.

**Debug**: F4 gains pool use, emitters, spawns dropped (warn once). **Test**: a **Particle Test**
(`--test=particles`, `--particles=burst|fountain|soft|events|budget`), with readback checks: a burst
of N gives N live particles, none outlive their lifetime, an overflow warns once.

### 2.12 VFX from animation events

The roadmap's promise: "a spell's burst comes from the clip instead of a timer". v0.8's `.events`
files already say *when*; a binding says *what*, with no game code:

```cpp
auto& vfx = fighter.AddComponent<ParticleEventComponent>();
vfx.Bind("step_l", leftFootDust, 6);            // an instant: a burst of 6
vfx.Bind("step_r", rightFootDust, 6);
vfx.BindRange("hitbox", swordTrail);            // a range: the emitter plays while it is open
```

`AnimationSystem::Update` already copies each frame's events before any handler runs
(`EventScratch::Deliveries`); a `ParticleEventSystem` reads the same copy after scripts get theirs.
Emitters are ordinary entities, usually on sockets. A `RangeEnd` (including the ones a rebind or a
destroyed animator queues) stops a range's emitter. Editing a `.events` file while the game runs
moves the bursts, through v0.8's hot-reload.

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Vulkan can't run on the dev VM | Every Vulkan run on the GPU machine; or a software Vulkan driver (Mesa lavapipe) installed here, which needs the user's go-ahead |
| Clip distances or instanced shadow views misbehave on a backend | Checked per backend in P5; the per-view fallback (§2.5) is designed in |
| Shadow acne, peter-panning, shimmer, cube seams | Slope + normal-offset bias, sphere-fit and texel-snapped cascades, wider-than-90° faces; the Shadow Test's `acne` mode and pan diff |
| Shadow cost grows with vertices × views (no per-mesh culling until v1.0) | The views are capped (4 cascades + the local budget); timed per room in P6 and W2 |
| Volatile UBO writes (Vulkan `maxVersions`) | One `ShadowViews` and one `ShadowData` write a scene; post settings in one UBO a frame; spawn records in one upload; each sized like `SkinData` |
| 16F blending isn't bit-identical to 8-bit | Post-off is the 0 px gate; post-on `None` ≤ 1/255 is documented |
| Storage-buffer bindings on D3D11 and Vulkan offsets | The Compute Test reads back on all three in P9, before particles build on it |
| Readback stalls | A ring of staging resources; a result is read only once its fence passed |
| Framebuffer resize keeps objects alive in place | Covered by the Render Target Test; the old behaviour is a latent dangling pointer today |
| Tracy merged into `DingoEngine.lib` through `BundledVendorLibs` | Built in CI with `--profile`; Tracy stays out of public headers |
| Scope (~33 engine days) | Cut order in §3; examples get their own plans |

### P3 (2026-10-08)

- **API as designed** (`Graphics/PostProcess.h`: `ToneMapOperator`, `ToneMapSettings`, `PostProcessSettings`, `PostProcessStack`; `Renderer::GetPostProcessStack()`; `PostProcessComponent`). `BloomSettings` and `AmbientOcclusionSettings` join `PostProcessSettings` in P4 and P8, not before.
- **Scene targets** are cached per output size rather than one resized per frame, because two scenes of a frame (the test viewport and a minimap, or the Post Test's strips and its viewport) would otherwise resize one back and forth. Each target has its own tone-map material (one shared shader), since swapping a material's texture rebuilds its pipelines.

Values with a max channel between 0.8 and 1.0 *do* change slightly (identity stops at the knee, as §2.3 says), so "unclipped pixels keep their v0.8 values" holds below 0.8 only.
- **`FullscreenShader` + `DrawFullscreen`** (`Graphics/FullscreenPass.h`) instead of P2's planned `FullscreenPass`: a shader shared by several materials is what the per-target tone map needs.
- **Post Test** (`--test=post`; `--tonemap=`, `--exposure=`, `--post-off`): the scene, five gradient strips, and seven readback checks (rising curves, Soft's identity and white point, None, hue, None vs off within 1/255, disabled Begin exactly equal to off). `--post=bloom|ao|chain` aren't there yet; bloom arrives with P4.
- **Lighting Test `--post`** (and a checkbox) runs its scene through the default Soft curve, for the `overbudget` clipped-vs-Soft pair the visual log wants.
- **Docs**: `docs/post-processing.md`; lighting.md's Limits and scenes-and-ecs.md's component table updated.
- **Not verified here** (as P1/P2): no build, run or capture. Checked: clang syntax over engine and test sources; glslang + SPIRV-Cross (SM 5.0/5.1) over `PostToneMap.glsl` and the test's gradient shader. The 0 px "post off" gate and the Soft readback are owed on the GPU machine.

### P4 (2026-10-08)

- **As designed**: `BloomSettings { Enabled, Intensity 0.5, Threshold 1.0, Knee 0.1, Radius 1.0 }` in `PostProcessSettings`; six R11G11B10F levels from half resolution per scene target; 13-tap downsample with Karis' average on the first, 3x3 tent upsample added level by level; the sum added before the tone curve. One shader file, `PostBloom.glsl`, built three ways by define.
- **The threshold is hard**: zero at or below `Threshold`, a quadratic over `Knee` *above* it joining `excess - knee/2` with slope 1 (Unity's soft knee straddles the threshold, which would let a 0.95 pixel glow and break "a 0–1 scene gains nothing"). `Knee` is in brightness units, not a fraction.
- **Bindings**: the tone map gains texture/sampler slot 1 (bindings 3/4) and `ToneMapData.Bloom`. With bloom off the slot holds a 1x1 black texture and the intensity is 0, so the arithmetic is `x + 0 * 0` and P3's output is unchanged.
- **Post Test**: `--post=bloom`/`--bloom`, panel sliders, and three readback checks (a 0..1 gradient byte-identical with and without bloom; an 8 x 8 square at 8 stays inside its edge without bloom; with it, it glows 4 and 8 px out, fading, alike on both sides). **Lighting Test**: `--bloom` (implies `--post`) for the `materials` lamp pair.
- **Not verified here**: as before, no build, run or capture; glslang + SPIRV-Cross over all three bloom variants, the tone map and the spot shader, clang over the C++.

### P5 (2026-10-08)

- **As designed** for the sun: `CastShadows`/`ShadowStrength` appended to `DirectionalLight` and `DirectionalLightComponent`; `Renderer3DShadowSettings` (as §2.6, plus `DebugCascades`) in `Renderer3DParams::Shadows`, `SetShadowSettings` clamping; `ShadowCasting { On, Off, ShadowsOnly }` on both mesh components and as the last argument of `SubmitMesh`/`SubmitSkinnedMesh`; batches keyed by (material, casting). One D32 atlas (made on the first casting scene), tiles from a power-of-two allocator, every casting batch drawn once instanced over the cascades with clip distances at the tile edges (`Renderer3D_Shadow.glsl`, vertex-only); the per-view fallback of §2.5 wasn't needed on paper and isn't written.
- **`EndScene` order** changed: every chunk is uploaded first, then the shadow pass, then the lit draws. A scene without a casting light skips the shadow pass and takes no shadow branch in the shader; its pixels should be unchanged, which the GPU machine has to confirm (the lit shader's SPIR-V changes: the include, the guarded multiply).
- **Skinned**: `Skinning.glsl` holds `SkinData`, `SkinMatrix` and `SkinNormal`, included by the lit shader (same maths, moved) and by the shadow shader's `DE_SKINNED` variant. The instance budget is resolved once per scene (`ResolveSkinnedBudget`) for both passes; the skin buffer's write budget is `2 x MaxSkinnedInstances`.
- **Bindings**: `ShadowData` (b5), `u_ShadowAtlas` (t6), `u_ShadowSampler` (s7) in `Shadows.glsl`, overridable by `DE_SHADOW_*_BINDING` defines, found by name (`Shader::FindTextureBinding`/`FindSamplerBinding` are new; `Material::SetShadowResources`, part of the pass cache key only when the shader declares them). `ShadowData` is written every `EndScene`, since the lit shader always binds it; a 1x1 placeholder depth texture stands in for the atlas until it exists.
- **Comparison is `Less`** (P2): with `DepthBias` 4, `SlopeBias` 2 and `NormalBias` 1.5 texels as defaults (the plan named the knobs, not values). Vulkan applies the slope bias only because the constant is non-zero.
- **Cascades**: practical split, sphere fit with the radius rounded up to 1/16 m, texel snap of the projected world origin, depth range from the casters' AABB (skinned casters: rest pose, padded by half its size) to the slice's far side. An orthographic camera gets one cascade. The last cascade fades to lit over its `CascadeBlend`, so shadows end softly at `MaxDistance`.
- **Debug**: F4 "Renderer3D shadows" (cascades and where they end, casters, atlas draws, the tint checkbox, an atlas viewer through ImGui), GPU timer `Shadows`.
- **Shadow Test** (`--test=shadow`, `--shadow=sun|acne|skinned`, `--shadow-cascades`, `--shadow-pan`) with six readback checks; `spot|point|budget|probe` are P6/P7's. **Animation Test** `--anim-shadow` (a default sun, which lights exactly like its default light, casting).
- **Docs**: `docs/shadows.md`; lighting.md (Limits, `GetLightAttenuation`'s occlusion note), animation.md (skinned shadows shipped), scenes-and-ecs.md (the new fields).
- **Not verified here**: no build, run or capture (Linux, no MSVC/GPU). glslang + SPIRV-Cross (SM 5.0/5.1) over both lit and both shadow variants: the HLSL has `SV_InstanceID` (no base-instance cbuffer), `SV_ClipDistance0` and `SampleCmpLevelZero`. Owed on the GPU machine: the checks on all three backends, no acne on the grazing plane, the slow-pan shimmer diff, the 0 px gate for non-casting scenes, and the shadow pass's GPU time. First suspects if something is off: D3D11 sampling the typeless atlas through a comparison sampler, the zero-colour-attachment pipeline, and instance/clip-distance support in the vertex-only pipeline.

### P6 (2026-10-08)

- **As designed**: `CastShadows`/`ShadowStrength` appended to `PointLight`, `SpotLight` and both components; `Renderer3DCapabilities::MaxShadowedLocalLights` (8, at most 16); the drawn casting lights take slots in the budget's order; a spot is one perspective tile, a point six cube faces each a little wider than 90 degrees; tiles by rank (1024 / 512 / 256) held until the rank moves two places; a light past the slots lights unshadowed (warn once, `Statistics::UnshadowedLights`, F4).
- **Additions**: a spot light wider than 75 degrees casts through six faces (one perspective view can't hold it); a casting light whose range touches no caster takes no slot; a full atlas halves a light's tiles down to 64 before giving up (warn once). `Renderer3DShadowSettings::LocalShadowResolution` (1024) sets the top tier, and a point light's faces take half a spot's size.
- **Data**: `ShadowData` is now `Tiles[100]` (the cascades first, then each light's one or six) plus `LocalShadows[32]` by light slot (first tile, count, strength; the light's position for the cube face and the normal offset), 10688 B; the shadow pass's `ShadowViews` holds 100 views, every casting batch still one instanced draw over all of them. The lit shader's local loop multiplies `LocalLightShadow` only while `ShadowCounts.w > 0`, so a scene without casting local lights takes no new branch. `Shadows.glsl`'s `SampleCascade` became `SampleShadowTile` (which also returns lit behind a perspective view, `w <= 0`).
- **#75 rank fade, opt-in**: `Renderer3DCapabilities::LightBudgetFade` (`SetLightBudgetFade`), **0 by default**, because D2 (every new feature opt-in, 0 px for existing games) outranks "fixed": the Lighting Test's `overbudget` scene is in the 0 px set and any fade changes it. With a band above 0 the budget ranks by one continuous priority (score / (1 + distance / range)) instead of score-then-nearness, since a fade needs a value that is continuous across a swap; each drawn light is scaled by a smoothstep of its priority over the first dropped light's, so the two are both dark when they trade places. Shadow slots hand over through the same band (strength, not colour). Frustum entry can still change the ranking at once (documented). The Lighting Test takes `--budget-fade[=band]`.
- **Shadow Test**: `--shadow=spot|point|budget` with nine new checks (tile counts, the box and four pillars casting on four cube faces, the open floor unchanged across the seams and on the -y face, 8 of 12 shadowed, a still scene drawn twice identically, `ToLight` carrying the fields, the fade dimming lights at the edge on a private renderer). `probe` is P7's.
- **Not verified here**: no build, run or capture (Linux, no MSVC/GPU). glslang + SPIRV-Cross (SM 5.0/5.1) over both lit and both shadow variants; clang over the engine and test sources. Owed on the GPU machine: the checks on all three backends, the cube seams (the diagonal floor points), perspective-depth acne on the point light's floor, the 0 px gate for every non-casting scene (the lit shader gained a branch), and the shadow pass's GPU time with 8 point lights (48 views).

### P7 (2026-10-08)

- **As designed**, with the light named differently: Renderer3D has no light handles, so `GetLastSubmittedLight()` returns a `ShadowProbeLight` (directional index, or the point/spot light's submission index) for the latest accepted `SubmitLight`, and `AddShadowProbe(light, point, key)` takes it with a caller-chosen 64-bit key; `GetShadowProbeResult(key)` returns the latest answer (`std::optional`). The Scene API is as designed: `GetLightVisibility(light, point, key)` and `GetShadowedLightAttenuation(light, point, key)`; `LightSystem::SubmitLights` maps each probed entity to its `ShadowProbeLight` as it submits it.
- **GPU**: one fullscreen draw into a 256 x 1 R8 target per scene that has GPU probes, after the atlas pass, evaluating `DirectionalShadow` / `LocalLightShadow` (the lit shader's functions) with a zero normal; `ReadPixels` records the copy at that point, so the next scene reuses the target. Answers arrive at the next `BeginFrame` (one frame for `Renderer3D`, two through `Scene`, which pulls answers at its next `SubmitLights`). Probes of lights without a shadow are answered on the CPU at once.
- **No normal offset**: a probe is a point, not a surface, so the docs say to probe in the air. The probe UBO goes through the material's scene-buffer slot (binding 0) on a buffer of its own with `k_MaxScenesPerFrame` writes.
- **Shadow Test** `--shadow=probe` (the sweep drawn as red-to-green spheres) and six checks run over the 8 frames after start: behind the sun's box, the spot light's box and a point light's pillar 0, in the open 1, an uncast light 1 on the first frame, a sweep across the sun shadow's edge 1 → 0 with values in between, and `Scene::GetLightVisibility` behind the ECS scene's `ShadowsOnly` box.
- **Not verified here**: no build or run. glslang + SPIRV-Cross over `Renderer3D_ShadowProbe.glsl`; clang over the engine and test. Owed on the GPU machine: the six probe checks on all three backends (R8 render target and readback are new to this path), and the latency (answers expected within 2 frames).

### P8 (2026-10-08)

- **As designed**: `AmbientOcclusionSettings { Enabled, Radius 0.5, Intensity 1, Power 1.5, Bias 0.02, HalfResolution }` in `PostProcessSettings`; SAO's 12 spiral taps (7 turns, the paper's per-pixel spin hash) at half resolution into R8, normals from depth with the nearer of each neighbour pair, a two-pass depth-aware blur (the paper's 9 weights, taps dropped by relative view-depth difference), then a multiply on the HDR colour before bloom and the tone curve.
- **The projection**: `PostProcessStack::Begin` gained `Begin(settings, projection)`; `SceneRenderer` passes the camera's projection. Without one AO is skipped with one warning; the other passes run as before.
- **The multiply** is a new `BlendMode::Multiply` (colour `DstColor`/`Zero`, alpha `Zero`/`One`), drawn into the scene target after the passes that sample its depth (the scene target is unbound while they draw).
- **Before particles**: `PostProcessStack::ApplyAmbientOcclusion()` applies AO once per `Begin`; `End` does it if nothing has. P10's particle pass calls it before drawing, so particles aren't darkened.
- **Tests**: the Post Test draws a room (floor, wall, a box on the floor and one floating 0.6 m before the wall) through the chain with None, with and without AO, and checks the open floor within 2/255, the crease darker by 5 % or more, and every wall pixel along the floating box's row unchanged right up to its silhouette. `--post=ao` shows the room. Lighting Test `--ao`. The plan's "Shadow Test room" pair is the Post Test room instead.
- **Not verified here**: no build or run. glslang + SPIRV-Cross over the three variants; clang over the engine and tests. Owed on the GPU machine: the three checks on all backends (the typeless depth SRV through a point sampler, the multiply blend on RGBA16F), the GPU time (`AO`), and a look at the half-resolution upsample along silhouettes.

### P9 (2026-10-08)

- **Compute**: `#type compute` through shaderc and SPIRV-Cross to `cs_5_0`/`cs_5_1` (the HLSL target and stage parsing were there; `ConvertShaderTypeToNVRHI` gained `Compute`). The plan's `ComputePipeline` is a `ComputePass` (pipeline plus bindings, like `RenderPass`), run by `Renderer::Dispatch` / `CommandList::Dispatch`; NVRHI's automatic barriers order it against draws.

### P10 (2026-10-08)

- **As designed**: `ParticleEffectParams` (shape, rate, `BurstOnPlay`, lifetime and speed ranges, gravity, drag, inherited velocity, curl noise, size and spin over life, four HDR colour keys, additive or alpha, a sprite with flipbook, soft distance, capacity) and one pool per `Renderer3D` (`MaxParticles` 65,536) with a ring per emitter, CPU-owned heads, emit and simulate kernels, an instanced draw. `ParticleEmitterComponent` and `ParticleEmitterRuntime` as designed; `Scene::EmitParticles`/`EmitParticlesAt`; "Play/Stop" is the component's `Playing` (and `ParticleEmitter::SetPlaying`).
- **Emitters are `std::shared_ptr`s**: `Renderer3D::CreateParticleEmitter` returns one, and the last reference returns the ring; the free list (`Internal::ParticlePool`) is shared so an emitter may outlive its renderer. Bursts (`Emit`, `EmitAt`) queue on the emitter and spawn at its next submission.
- **One kernel launch per scene, not per emitter**: simulate runs over all the scene's slots and emit over all its new particles, each thread finding its emitter (or spawn record) by binary search on prefix offsets. **Draws per batch**: emitters are sorted by (blend, sprite) and each run is one draw, the run's first emitter and slot in its material's UBO.
- **Soft particles** read a copy of the scene depth (`PostProcessStack::CopySceneDepth`, an R32F target made by a fullscreen copy) rather than the depth itself, which is bound for the depth test while particles draw. Without the post chain they draw hard.
- **Billboards** take the camera's right and up from the view-projection's first two rows (exact for the engine's symmetric projections), since Renderer3D keeps only the view-projection.
- **`GraphicsBuffer::ReadBack`** is new (the test counts live particles by reading the pool), with `Texture::ReadPixels`' timing.
- **Not done**: depth-buffer collision (the plan's stretch goal), particle sorting, and the F4 live editor of D7 (P12).
- **Particle Test** (`--test=particles`, `--particles=fountain|burst|soft|budget`; `events` is P11's): ten checks over five frames on a private renderer, reading the pool back (whole pool alive and a second emitter refused, spawn count, released rings return, a burst of 100 alive, ring overflow keeps 64 and counts 36, none outlive their lifetime, the rest age correctly, a component's burst through the SceneRenderer, soft against hard by readback at two heights).
- **Not verified here**: no build or run. glslang + SPIRV-Cross (SM 5.0/5.1) over both kernels, the draw and the depth copy; clang over the engine, test and examples. Owed on the GPU machine: the Particle Test on all three backends, the 64k-particle timing (target ≤ 0.3 ms for 10k), and a look at the fountain through bloom.
