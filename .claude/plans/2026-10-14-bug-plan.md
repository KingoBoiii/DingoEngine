## Phases

### P1 — #199: reserve the instancing budget before culling

Where it is today (`src/DingoEngine/Graphics/Renderer3D.cpp`):

- `GroupInstances` decides `InstanceCopies::Instanced` before culling (around `:1069-1098`). That
  part is right and stays.
- It then builds `InstanceGroup`s only for submissions that have a `Slot` after culling (`:1100-1124`).
- It gives the budget out in group order: forced groups first, then the rest (`:1134-1163`).

Each group costs:

| Group | Writes |
|---|---|
| Lit slot | 1, plus 1 when it casts and shadows are on |
| Shadow-only slot (a culled caster) | 1 |

Design to settle (Plan agent, `opus`, `high`; record the result here with `file:line` anchors):

1. **Reserve in submission order.** For each `InstanceCopies` entry marked `Instanced`, reserve an
   upper bound on the writes its groups can take. A copies entry is per `MaterialBatch` and mesh, and
   its submissions can reach slots with different `ShadowCasting`, so the bound must hold whatever
   culling does:
   - 1 lit write, when any copy draws lit;
   - 1 shadow write for the lit slot, when any copy casts and shadows are on;
   - 1 shadow-only write, when culling is on and any copy casts.
2. **Decide which copies fit before culling.** Forced copies go first, then the rest. An entry that
   doesn't fit is un-instanced: CPU batch, or dropped when forced, with the existing warning.
3. **After culling, groups spend only what their entry reserved.** Decide whether the unspent part of
   a reservation goes back to `m_InstanceDrawsThisFrame` for later scenes. Returning it would bring
   culling back into later scenes' outcome, so keeping it spent is probably right.
4. **Check the cost to the 512 budget.** The reservation is an upper bound, so near the limit fewer
   groups instance than today. Measure with `--throughput` in a dense scene, and say in
   `docs/performance.md` what the budget now counts.
5. **Docs.** `docs/performance.md` and `.claude/reference/rendering.md` (the instancing paragraph)
   say that past the budget, which meshes are instanced depends on submission order and on earlier
   scenes in the frame, never on the camera.

Test: `test/src/Tests/Renderer/Renderer3DBatchTest.cpp`, beside the three-scene growth step.

1. Submit 520 distinct meshes of 8 copies each.
2. Submit one more 8-copy mesh last in submission order, fully in view.
3. Render the view twice in one frame, with culling off and then on.
4. Check that the last mesh's `InstancedMeshes` contribution is the same both times.

Needs a real GPU: the batch test on Vulkan, DX11 and DX12.

#### As built (P1)

- `Renderer3D::GroupInstances` (`src/DingoEngine/Graphics/Renderer3D.cpp:1043`) books the budget for
  each `InstanceCopies` entry marked `Instanced`, in submission order, before it builds any group.
  A `BatchKey` carries `Shadows` and `ShadowGroups`, so an entry casts one way and reaches at most
  one lit slot and one shadow-only slot:
  - `LitWrites`: 1 for a lit batch, +1 when it casts (its lit slot's shadow draw).
  - `CasterWrites`: 1 when it casts (the shadow-only group of its culled casters).
  Every group is covered by a booking, so the frame's real writes never pass what was booked.
- Point 2 changed after review. A forced entry books only its `LitWrites` first, since they have no
  CPU path. Its `CasterWrites` book with the rest in submission order; when they don't fit, its
  culled casters cast from the CPU (`CastersInstanced`). Booking the whole entry first made 200
  casting meshes of an `Instancing` material, which drew before at 400 writes, drop 30 of them.
  Every other entry books both or neither, so a mesh stays on one path. A forced entry whose lit
  draws don't fit is `Dropped` (`DroppedInstancedMeshes`). `InstanceGroup::Forced` is gone.
- Point 1 changed: the shadow-only write is booked whether culling is on or off, so the culling
  toggle changes nothing either. It costs one unspent write per casting entry with culling off, a
  debug setting.
- Point 3: unspent bookings stay spent.
- Point 4: a casting mesh now books 3 writes where it used 2, so near the limit about 170 casting
  meshes instance instead of about 256. The Throughput Test's `repeat` and `mixed` scenes use 5
  instanced draws, far below it.
- Point 5: `docs/performance.md`, `docs/scenes-and-ecs.md`, `.claude/reference/rendering.md`, the
  `k_MaxInstancedDrawsPerFrame` doc and the `AssertOnOverflow` doc say what the budget counts.
  They also say that culling never changes it, but `shadows` turning on or off does. With only
  local shadow lights, the camera culling the light turns shadows off, and a caster then books 1.
- Test: three cases in `Renderer3DBatchTest.cpp`, after the three-scene step. Each runs with culling
  on and with it off, one frame per run, since every scene of a renderer shares the budget:
  - 520 distinct quads × 8 behind the camera, then 8 boxes in view: the boxes batch on the CPU.
  - The same with the `Instancing` material: the boxes are dropped.
  - 170 casting quads under a shadow light: the casting boxes after them batch on the CPU.
  The old code instanced the boxes with culling on.
