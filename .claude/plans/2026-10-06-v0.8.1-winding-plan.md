# v0.8.1 — winding and culling fixes (GitHub #68, #69)

Two open issues, both filed 2026-10-02 from Headstone (KingoBoiii/Headstone#49):

- **#68** `Material` ignores `MaterialParams::FrontCounterClockwise`.
- **#69** `Mesh::CreateSphere` winds its triangles inward.

Together they mean no `Material` can cull back faces of engine meshes correctly today.

## Decision needed before step 4

**Default of `MaterialParams::FrontCounterClockwise`.**

| Option | Effect | Breaks |
|---|---|---|
| **B. `true` (recommended)** | `MaterialParams()` + its default `CullMode::Back` culls correctly for boxes, spheres (after #69) and every assimp model, out of the box. Removes the "MaterialParams culls the visible faces" trap the v0.7 lighting plan worked around. | Anyone culling with `CullMode::Front` as a workaround turns inside out — in practice only Headstone. |
| A. keep `false` | Zero behaviour change. | Nothing, but the trap stays: `CullMode::Back` (the default) still draws every engine mesh inside out unless the user also sets `FrontCounterClockwise(true)`. |

`PipelineParams::FrontCounterClockwise` stays `false` either way: its only users (Renderer2D batches, the raw-pipeline tests) don't cull.

## Facts checked (2026-10-06, on `2b834fc`)

1. **#68 confirmed.** `src/DingoEngine/Graphics/Material.cpp:182-188` builds `PipelineParams` without `.SetFrontCounterClockwise(...)`. `NvrhiPipeline.cpp:66` already honours it.
2. **#69 confirmed.** `Mesh.cpp:163-164`: `(i2 − i0) × (i1 − i0)` at θ = 90°, φ = 0 is `(−d², 0, 0)`, inward. The issue's order `(i0, i1, i2)`, `(i1, i3, i2)` gives `(+d², 0, 0)` for both triangles.
3. **The backends agree.** NVRHI's Vulkan backend flips the viewport to D3D coordinates (`VKViewportWithDXCoords`, negative height, `vendor/nvrhi/src/vulkan/vulkan-graphics.cpp:616`), so one flag means the same on Vulkan, DX11 and DX12. This matches the issue's Headstone evidence. **`docs/lighting.md:303` is wrong**: it says lit materials draw both faces "because front-face winding differs between Vulkan and D3D". The real cause was these two bugs.
4. **Lit draws can't change.** `CreateLitMaterial` (`Renderer3D.cpp:591`) and the skinned twin (`:463`) force `CullMode::None`, and no shader reads `gl_FrontFacing`. Every example draws lit or `CullMode::None`, so all examples stay pixel-identical.
5. **In-repo culling materials:** only `Mesh3DTest` and `Model3DTest` (`CullMode::Back`). Today the box and every model draw inside out there and only the sphere looks right. `Model.cpp:164`'s per-submesh materials have no shader and are never drawn.
6. **Physics side effect of #69 (a fix):** triangle meshes are one-sided by CCW winding (`docs/physics-3d.md:118`). A `CreateSphere` used as a non-convex `MeshCollider3DComponent` lets bodies fall through from outside today and becomes solid after #69. `MeshColliderTest`'s pebbles are `Convex = true` and unaffected.
7. **This worktree is one commit behind `origin/master`** (`853a9b6`, docs only).

## Steps

One commit per fix, each with a why-body and a "Verified:" paragraph, made only on "commit". Captures follow `reference_dingo_build_and_capture` (private desktop, PrintWindow, 3D viewport crop, `test/imgui.ini` restored afterwards).

0. **Set up** (~15 min). Sync with `master`, branch `v0.8.1`, bootstrap submodules from the main checkout, `premake5 vs2026`, build the Debug test app. Copy it to `build/before-v0.8.1/`.

1. **`test(renderer): add mesh flags and a winding check to the Mesh 3D Test`** (~20 min)
   - `--mesh=box|sphere` and `--mesh-angle=<deg>` (freezes the turn, like `--model-angle`).
   - A startup check, logged `PASS`/`FAIL` like the Lighting Test's: for `CreateBox` and `CreateSphere` at (16,16), (6,8), (3,4), every triangle with non-zero area has `dot(faceNormal, centroid) > 0`. Pole triangles are degenerate; skip them.
   - Build this into the "before" exe too. Expected: box `PASS`, sphere `FAIL`.

2. **`fix(graphics): pass FrontCounterClockwise from Material to its pipeline` (#68)** (~5 min code, ~30 min verify)
   - Add `.SetFrontCounterClockwise(m_Params.FrontCounterClockwise)` to the chain in `Material::GetOrCreateRenderPass`. The cache key needs nothing: params are fixed per material.
   - Verify on Vulkan, DX11, DX12: `--test=mesh --mesh=box|sphere --mesh-angle=30` and `--test=model --model-angle=30` are 0 px against "before".
   - Control: temporarily `SetFrontCounterClockwise(true)` in `Mesh3DTest` → the box turns right side out (large diff). This is the proof the flag now lands. Revert.

3. **`fix(graphics): wind CreateSphere counter-clockwise like CreateBox` (#69)** (~5 min code, ~20 min verify)
   - `Mesh.cpp:163-164` → `i0, i1, i2` / `i1, i3, i2`.
   - Verify: the winding check reads `PASS` for every sphere. `--test=light --entities` and a frozen Candlewick and Marionette frame are 0 px (spheres drawn lit). The mesh test's sphere now draws inside out like the box: expected under the old default, fixed in step 4.

4. **`fix(graphics)!: treat counter-clockwise as front by default`** (~5 min code, ~30 min verify) — **option B**
   - `Material.h:21` → `FrontCounterClockwise = true`.
   - Verify on all three backends: box, sphere and the Duck/Fox draw right side out. Before/after images of each go to the user.
   - **Option A instead:** keep the default, and set `SetFrontCounterClockwise(true)` in `Mesh3DTest` and `Model3DTest`.

5. **`docs: record the v0.8.1 winding fixes`** (~20 min)
   - `docs/lighting.md:302-303`: drop the false backend reason. Lit materials draw both faces because the lit shader has no back-face handling, not because the APIs disagree.
   - A short "Winding and culling" note next to the custom-material section of `docs/scenes-and-ecs.md`: engine meshes and assimp models are CCW seen from outside; `MaterialParams` culls back faces by default.
   - `docs/physics-3d.md:118`: a `CreateSphere` mesh collider is solid from outside since v0.8.1.
   - `CLAUDE.md` rendering notes, `ROADMAP.md` v0.8.1 entry, `VERSION` 0.8.1.

6. **Review** (~10 min). Fresh Sonnet agent reviews the uncommitted diff before any commit.

7. **Release** (~30 min plus the master CI run), per `project_release_process`: PR `v0.8.1` → `master` with `Fixes #68` and `Fixes #69` in the body, merge, wait for "Build master branch", lightweight tag `v0.8.1`, `gh release create` by hand, check all 13 assets.

**Total:** about 2.5–3 hours, most of it captures on three backends.

## As built (2026-10-06)

These notes override the design above where they differ.

- **Decision: option B.** The user chose to default to `true`, and also asked to fix the Model 3D Test's models. They were inside out for the same reason (`CullMode::Back` with the flag dropped), so steps 2 and 4 fix them with no test-side change.
- **`PipelineParams` had the field but no setter**, so step 2 also adds `PipelineParams::SetFrontCounterClockwise`.
- **`PipelineParams::FrontCounterClockwise` must stay `false`.** Fact 5 and the out-of-scope note were wrong about Renderer2D: its text pass takes the default `CullMode::Back` with clockwise fronts, so it does cull.
- **The Renderer3D.h comment on `CreateLitMaterial`** repeated the false "winding differs between Vulkan and D3D" reason, so it was reworded with the docs.
- **The CPU winding check can't catch a regression of #68.** That one is covered by the captures and the control build.
- **Captures**: hidden desktop, 3D viewport crop 300,24,1005,872 of the 1600×900 client, `--mesh-angle=30` / `--model-angle=30`, on Vulkan, D3D11 and D3D12.

  - the CPU-only check, noted above.

## Out of scope

- **Headstone follow-up** (separate repo, after v0.8.1 ships): bump the engine, switch its `CullMode::Front` workaround to `Back` (option B), and let the glow material cull again (Headstone#49).
- Letting `CreateLitMaterial` honour a caller's cull mode: possible once these land, but it's a feature, not part of either issue.
- `CullMode::BackAndFront` maps to `None` in `NvrhiPipeline.cpp:38` (the comment there says "back culling"). Harmless today: only Renderer2D and the raw-pipeline tests use it.
