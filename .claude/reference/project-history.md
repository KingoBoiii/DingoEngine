# Project map, issues and version history

Read on demand from CLAUDE.md. The full source tree, where issues and reviews live, and what each release contained (the plan itself is ROADMAP.md).

## Project structure

```
include/DingoEngine/       Public API headers (Core, Graphics, Events, Windowing,
                           Physics/2D, Physics/3D, Scene, UI, Audio, Asset)
src/DingoEngine/           Implementations, incl. backend-only code:
  Graphics/NVRHI/          NVRHI wrappers (Vulkan/, DirectX11/, DirectX12/)
  Graphics/Shaders/        engine GLSL (Renderer3D_Lit.glsl), embedded into the lib
  Physics/2D/Box2D/        the only box2d.h includer
  Physics/3D/JoltPhysics/  the only Jolt includer
  Scene/SceneData.h        the scene PIMPL; EnTT stays inside src/Scene/
  Scene/Systems/           ScriptSystem, PhysicsSync, AudioSync, CameraUtils,
                           LightSystem — the systems Scene delegates to
                           (engine-internal)
  Asset/AssetManagerData.h the AssetManager PIMPL — worker thread, mutexes and
                           the asset maps live here, not in the public header
vendor/                    Third-party submodules — NEVER modify vendor code
examples/                  FlappyBird, Breakout3D, SpaceInvaders (Scene/ECS showcase),
                           AngryBirds (2D physics), DungeonCrawler (top-down 2D slice),
                           DungeonCrawler3D (ECS-integrated 3D, procedural dungeons,
                           script-driven, custom materials), EchoVault (v0.5 showcase:
                           character controller, casts, 3D positional audio, gamepad;
                           v0.7: orbs and sentries carry point lights),
                           ArenaShooter (v0.6 showcase: AssetManager, async load,
                           hot-reload), Candlewick (v0.7 showcase: stealth, lantern
                           oil, spot-light vision cones tested with
                           GetLightAttenuation, light LOD, lit braziers;
                           v0.9: shadows you hide in via shadow probes,
                           bloom, particle flames, AO),
                           Marionette (v0.8 showcase: melee duel, combat windows
                           from clip events, socketed hit spheres, masked block
                           layer, five clip libraries retargeted onto four
                           characters, three AI tiers, live .events editing;
                           v0.9: VFX from clip events, impact sparks,
                           moonlit skinned shadows, bloom, AO)
test/                      Dingo-TestFramework — interactive graphics/renderer/asset
                           tests, one per feature under src/Tests/
docs/                      Per-feature docs (see Issues, docs and reviews below)
```

Vendor forks that upstream as CMake (e.g. box2d) carry their own `premake5.lua`, `include`d from the root workspace.



## Issues, docs and reviews

- **Known bugs are GitHub issues** (since 2026-10-06): `bug` for defects and latent bugs, `enhancement` for limitations. `KNOWN-BUGS.md` is gone: the K-numbers plans and reviews cite are issues #73–#83, each with its K-number at the end of its title (K10 → #73 … K25 → #83); K1–K9, K12–K15 and K20 were fixed before the move (`git log -- KNOWN-BUGS.md`), and K18, K19 and K21–K24 in v0.8.3.
- **v0.9's open findings are issues #111–#143**, filed 2026-10-08: the review's (#111–#142, the review ID at the end of each title), the missing per-light caster exclusion (#137) and *Candlewick*'s two leftovers (#143), the last two done on `feat/enhancement-physics3d-animation-loader`.
- **Engine gaps no milestone takes are `enhancement` issues** too: `ROADMAP-BACKLOG.md` is gone. Its unplanned items were #98 (game UI layer, still open) and #99–#104 (Marionette's six, done on `feat/enhancement-physics3d-animation-loader` with #104's root-motion design in `.claude/plans/2026-10-09-enhancement-plan.md`); the rest shipped by v0.8.2 or sit in ROADMAP.md's v1.0. Plans cite its items as `#1b`, `#4a`… — the last full version is `git show 5c2e057:ROADMAP-BACKLOG.md`.
- `docs/` — `getting-started`, `application-and-layers`, `scenes-and-ecs`, `rendering-2d`, `physics-2d`, `physics-3d`, `asset-pipeline`, `lighting`, `animation` (v0.8), `audio`, `distribution` (asset packages). Keep the relevant one in step when changing a public API.
- `.claude/reviews/` — dated code-review reports, findings keyed `B*`/`O*`/`R*` with a per-finding fix status. The current ones are `2026-10-08-v0.9.0-review.md` (the v0.9 engine work: every finding fixed, 3 High, 11 Medium, 24 Low and the test gaps, the last as issues #111–#136 and #138–#142, titled "(v0.9 review <ID>)"; only #137 and #143 stay open), `2026-10-03-v0.8.0-review.md` (the animation engine work: 7 Medium and 8 Low fixed, 11 Low open), `2026-10-04-marionette-review.md` (the v0.8 game), `2026-10-01-v0.7.0-review.md` (the lighting engine work) and `2026-10-01-candlewick-review.md` (the game). Take the *fix-status section*, not the summary sentence, as the state, and confirm against `git log` before believing either. `2026-07-29-v0.6.0-review.md` is closed out; read it before touching the asset/hot-reload path, where it also records why a passing "before" run in an A/B repro is suspect.

- **v0.7.0** (engine work and the *Candlewick* example done on the branch, P0–P8 of `.claude/plans/2026-09-26-v0.7-lighting-plan.md` and `.claude/plans/2026-10-01-candlewick-plan.md`, whose "As built" notes override their designs; reviewed in `.claude/reviews/2026-10-01-v0.7.0-review.md`; released 2026-10-01): the lighting above, the Lighting Test (`--test=light`, with PASS/FAIL checks), DungeonCrawler3D's opt-in `--night`, EchoVault's orbs and sentries as lit emissive materials carrying point lights, and `examples/Candlewick` (lantern radius = oil, warden spot-light cones, a game-side light LOD, braziers as checkpoints; flags `--room=1..4`, `--debug-cone`, `--no-light-lod`, `--light-budget=16..32`, `--freeze`, `--overview`).

The Tracy timeline capture was skipped. **F4 effect editor** (P12, D7): every `ParticleEffect` registers itself (`Internal::GetLiveParticleEffects`, main thread), and the Renderer tab's "Particle effect editor" edits one in place and copies `Internal::ParticleEffectToCode` (fluent C++, also logged).
- **Candlewick on v0.9** (P14): every gameplay light (lantern, braziers, warden lamps and eyes) casts, nothing else does, and `MaxShadowedLocalLights` is 16 (`GAMEPLAY_LIGHTS_MAX` 14 fits, `static_assert`). Lantern parts, warden lamp glass/cap, flame cores, wax, markers, debug meshes and the **player** are `ShadowCasting::Off` (detection probes from inside the player's body; since #137 the engine has per-light caster groups and probe clearance, which Candlewick doesn't use yet). Cut-away walls turn `ShadowsOnly`. `Wardens::ClampRange` and `--no-range-clamp` are gone (`Wardens::Arm` enables the eye once physics exists). `Detection`: cone samples by `GetShadowedLightAttenuation(eye, sample, key = sample)`, braziers in `IsFlameLit` by their shadow (`PROBE_KEY_CHEST`), the lantern beacon by `GetLightVisibility(lantern, eye, PROBE_KEY_LANTERN + warden)`; every probe is asked every frame, the sight ray stays. `Flames.h/.cpp` hold seven particle effects (KeepWorld owns them); `PostProcessComponent` on the keep camera (Soft, bloom 0.35, AO). New flags: `--fixed-dt` (implied 1/60 by `--freeze`), `--perf` (with GPU timer means), `--no-post`, `--no-shadows`, `--no-particles`, `--hide-check` (`--perf`/`--hide-check` update in the background).
- **Marionette on v0.9** (P15): `ArenaVfx` owns the arena's effects and the impact emitters. `Fighter::BuildVfx` (controlled fighters, from `BuildHitRig`) parents dust emitters to the `foot.l`/`foot.r` sockets and the feet, and a trail emitter to the weapon part at the blade's tip; a `ParticleEventComponent` binds `step_l`/`step_r`/`dash` bursts and the `hitbox` range. `Combat::Judge` keeps a `Decision::Contact` (`ContactPoint`, the touch test itself unchanged); `Commit` bursts hit/block sparks or the parry flash there, and a killing hit queues `KO_DUST_DELAY` dust (`ArenaVfx::Update`). The moon and the four braziers cast; flame cores and debug spheres don't; a seen-through occluder turns `ShadowsOnly` (parts that cast nothing hide). Both cameras get `PostProcessComponent` (Soft, bloom 0.3, AO). New flags `--no-post`, `--no-shadows`, `--no-particles`. VFX never write gameplay state: `--check` and `--tournament` must read as before.
